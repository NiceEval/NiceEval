---
title: "Record and Snapshot"
sidebarTitle: "Record"
description: "Learn the separate roles of the project SQLite Record and a portable RecordSnapshot."
---

Record stores Runs, Attempts, Attachments, Content, and Seals. The project's operational Store lives at `.niceeval/record/record.sqlite`; it can contain both work in progress and published facts.

Only sealed Runs are available to Inspection, `query`, and `view`. Published facts are immutable. Cache, credentials, coordination state, and OS-user Service state are not Record facts.

```sh
niceeval record snapshot --output ./snapshots/baseline.record-snapshot
niceeval query discover --record ./snapshots/baseline.record-snapshot
niceeval view --record ./snapshots/baseline.record-snapshot
```

Snapshot is the only portable input accepted by `--record`. Its command removes unpublished closure and verifies the exact Seal. SQLite copies, WAL files, checkpoint files, and arbitrary external files cannot substitute for it.

A Snapshot fixes its exact Seal. `query` and `view` do not migrate, watch, or refresh it. A Snapshot can still contain sensitive business data, so its sender owns permissions, redaction, and retention.

Record does not own presentation, statistics, or comparisons. Fixed Inspection operations close those read semantics before passing results to `query` or first-party `view`.
